Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Notcheur has no servers, no account and no analytics. Nothing you do in Notcheur is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What Notcheur collects

Nothing. Notcheur has no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers and no user accounts. We do not operate a server that Notcheur talks to.

What Notcheur accesses on your Mac

Notcheur works with data on your own machine. It stays there unless you use Apple's iCloud, in which case it syncs to your iCloud account — not to us.

  • Notes. Notes you write, including images you paste, are stored on your Mac. If you're signed in to iCloud they sync between your own Macs through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). We have no access to it. Without iCloud, notes stay local only.
  • Calendar. With your permission, Notcheur reads and creates events in the calendars already on your Mac using Apple's EventKit framework. Event data is read on demand to draw the calendar view. It is never copied off your Mac, stored by Notcheur, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Clipboard. Notcheur keeps a short in-memory history of what you copy so you can paste it again. It is not written to disk and is discarded when you quit. Items copied on your other Apple devices may appear via Universal Clipboard.
  • Music and Spotify. With your permission, Notcheur uses Apple Events to read the current track from Apple Music and Spotify and to send transport commands (play, pause, skip, seek, volume). It reads only what's needed to display the track and does not log or transmit your listening activity. Spotify album artwork is fetched from Spotify's own image URL.
  • Focus. Notcheur can turn on a system Focus by running a Shortcut you created yourself. It does not read your Focus status or your notifications.
  • Settings. Preferences such as hover delay and week start are stored locally on your Mac.

Network use

Notcheur makes network connections only for:

  • iCloud sync of your notes, to Apple's servers under your own account
  • Downloading album artwork for the currently playing Spotify track
  • App Store purchases, handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit

There is no other network activity.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Notcheur receives only whether an entitlement is active — it never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Notcheur is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because Notcheur stores nothing on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. Removing Notcheur's local data is done by deleting the app; notes synced to iCloud can be removed from your iCloud account through Apple's own controls.
